# Kestara — UI/UX Reference Notes

> **Status:** Draft  
> **Purpose:** Record inspiration sources named by the Project Owner. These are reference products, not design specifications or assets to copy.

## 1. Reference Products

| Reference | URL | Experience area to study | Kestara application |
|---|---|---|---|
| Glints | https://glints.com/id | Job discovery, job detail, career content | Vacancy discovery and career information |
| Duolingo | https://id.duolingo.com | Guided learning, clear progress, small learning steps | Micro-learning and assessment progress |
| Jobstreet | https://id.jobstreet.com | Job search, filters, company and vacancy information | Job listing, search, and company details |
| LinkedIn | https://www.linkedin.com | Professional identity, community, networking | Profile, portfolio, and discussion community |
| Jobseeker | https://www.jobseeker.com | CV preparation and career-document workflow | Profile completeness and portfolio/CV support |

## 2. What to Learn, Not Copy

- Observe information hierarchy and task flow.
- Observe how search, filters, and job details are organized.
- Observe how progress and learning steps are communicated.
- Observe how professional profiles present skills and work samples.
- Observe how community interactions preserve context.
- Observe responsive behavior and empty/error states.

Do not copy logos, proprietary illustrations, text, exact page layouts, or protected assets. Kestara must have its own Green Teal identity.

## 3. Evaluation Checklist

When reviewing a reference flow, record:
- User goal.
- Entry point.
- Number of steps.
- Information required.
- Main action and secondary actions.
- Feedback after the action.
- Mobile behavior.
- Accessibility concerns.
- Privacy implications.
- What should be adapted for Kestara.

## 4. Evidence Rule

These notes record the URLs and inspiration categories requested by the Project Owner. They do not claim that a live usability audit or current screenshot review has been completed. If the team performs a live reference review, add date, page/flow reviewed, observations, and screenshots captured by the team.
